One family, gathered around the Word

Living Word A.G Church is a Pentecostal congregation in Besant Nagar, Chennai — shepherding families, youth and children with the unchanging truth of Scripture and the warmth of real community.

Our Shepherd

Pastor Vincent & Family

Pastor Vincent leads Living Word A.G Church alongside his family, who stand with him in ministry, worship and prayer. Together they carry a heart for discipleship — walking with every member through seasons of joy and struggle alike.

Their home has long been an extension of the church itself: a place of hospitality, counsel and unwavering faith, poured out for the good of this congregation.

Our Congregation

A community that worships, learns and grows together

Sunday after Sunday, families of every generation gather under one roof — children, youth, parents and elders — united in worship and the teaching of God's Word.

Whether it's a Sunday service, a Wednesday gospel meeting or a Saturday fasting prayer, this congregation shows up for one another, believing that faith is best lived out in community.

Our Mission & Values

📖 The Word First

Every gathering is rooted in Scripture — taught, lived, and shared without compromise.
